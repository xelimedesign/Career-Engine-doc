A quick guide before you start.

What the app is for

Career Engine helps you write resumes and cover letters from your own career profile, track the jobs you apply to, and review each application before you send it.

Main features

  • Profile: your verified work history, skills, and education.
  • Jobs: save job links or descriptions and track their status.
  • Resumes and Cover Letters: build, edit, and export as PDF or Word.
  • Review: check documents for accuracy and fit before you apply.
  • Documents: all your saved resumes and letters in one place.

Where to start

1. Fill in your Profile.

2. Open Sticky Notes (or press Alt+S) and save a job.

3. Queue a resume or cover letter for that job.
